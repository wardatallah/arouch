Who We Are

Craftsmanship You Can Trust

At Garage Arouch, we've been restoring vehicles to their former glory for over two decades. Our team of certified technicians combines old-school craftsmanship with cutting-edge technology to deliver results that exceed expectations.

We treat every vehicle as if it were our own — because we understand how much your car means to you. Whether it's a fender bender or major collision damage, we handle it with the same level of precision and care.
